Considering the increasing adoption and impact of applications integrated with large language models (LLMs), the consistently high success rate of prompt injection attacks on state-of-the-art LLMs poses a growing threat.
In this paper, we present a systematized overview of prompt injection techniques against large language models.
Besides a theoretical introduction to prompt injection attacks, we classify prompt injection approaches into two groups.
We present case studies on both word-level attack prompt construction and token-level gradient-based attack prompt optimization.
Additionally, we discuss the fundamental problem of instruction-data integration in LLMs, which enables effective prompt injection attacks, and consider three defense approaches of different efficacy and applicability.
